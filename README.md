# Pocket Pilot

Pocket Pilot helps you manage your income, expenses, and recent transactions in one place. Easily monitor your spending, track financial trends, and stay in control of your budget with a clear, user-friendly interface.

> This may take 1-2 minutes to load on first visit since we are using free-plan of onrender to host our backend server. It goes to sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity.

visit the [live demo](http://localhost:5173/) to see it in action!
